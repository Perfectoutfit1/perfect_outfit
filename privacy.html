Privacy policy

Your details, kept private.

This page explains what we collect when you contact us through this website, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Who we are

Perfect Outfit Intl, a custom clothing manufacturer at Shahabpura, Sialkot, Punjab 51310, Pakistan. For anything about your data, email perfectoutfitintl@gmail.com or WhatsApp +92 324 6574840.

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms:

  • Quote request: your name, email and/or WhatsApp number, product type, quantity, project details, and optionally your brand name, country and a design file.
  • Price-sheet request (showcase page): your email and/or WhatsApp number and the style you asked about.
  • Meeting request: your name, brand name, preferred day and time, and topic.

Each form also records which page it was sent from. This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Why we use it

To reply to you, prepare your quote, and handle samples and orders you place with us. We never sell or rent your details, and we never resell, reuse or share your designs, patterns or artwork. We’re happy to sign an NDA before you send files.

Services involved

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers form submissions, including any file you attach, to our email inbox. FormSubmit keeps submissions for up to 30 days.
  • WhatsApp: meeting requests and “send on WhatsApp” buttons open WhatsApp on your device. Messages you send there are covered by WhatsApp’s own privacy policy.
  • Vercel hosts this website and Google Fonts serves its fonts. Like any website, these services receive your IP address and browser details so they can deliver the page.

How long we keep it

We keep your messages and files for as long as we need them to handle your enquiry or order and for our business records, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to show you the details we hold about you, correct them, or delete them. Email perfectoutfitintl@gmail.com and we’ll reply within a few working days.

Changes

If we change how we handle your details, we’ll update this page and the date above.
