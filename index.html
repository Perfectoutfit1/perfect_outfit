FAQ

Questions founders ask

What is your minimum order quantity?

Our MOQ starts at just 30 pieces per design.

How much does a sample cost?

It depends on your design — the fabric, construction and customization involved. We confirm the exact sample cost with your quote.

How long does bulk production take?

Bulk production takes 7–14 days after your sample is approved.

Where are you based?

We’ve been based in Sialkot, Pakistan since 2015 — one of the world’s best-known centres for sportswear, leather and cut-and-sew garments, which is why we can make all four product lines in-house. We ship door to door from Sialkot by DHL and FedEx.

Do I need a tech pack?

No. A mockup, reference photo or even a sketch is enough — our team will help you develop it into a production-ready spec.

Do you ship to my country?

Yes. We ship worldwide by DHL and FedEx express, door to door — including the USA, UK, Europe and Australia.

Will my designs stay private?

Always. We never resell, reuse or share client designs, and we're happy to sign an NDA before you send your files.
