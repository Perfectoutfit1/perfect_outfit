Our story

A Sialkot business, built for overseas brands.

Hussnain Awan
Founder · Since 2015

Hussnain Awan started Perfect Outfit Intl in Sialkot in 2015. Sialkot is one of the world’s best-known centres for sportswear, leather and cut-and-sew garments, and that’s where we make every order.

Today we produce four product lines: heavyweight streetwear, fully custom team uniforms, genuine leather garments and branded workwear. Everything is made to the buyer’s own design and finished under the buyer’s own label.

We’re set up for buyers overseas: a low 30-piece minimum so new labels can start small, samples in 7–10 days, regular WhatsApp updates during production, and door-to-door DHL and FedEx shipping.

How we work

What you can count on.

Your designs stay yours

We never resell, reuse or share your designs, patterns or artwork. We’ll sign an NDA before you send a file.

Sample before bulk

Nothing goes into bulk production until you approve a sample. If the sample doesn’t match the spec you approved, we remake it free.

You see the progress

We reply within 24 hours, then send WhatsApp photos and updates from cutting and stitching through to packing.

Start small, then scale

From 30 pieces per design, so you can test a drop before committing to a big run.
